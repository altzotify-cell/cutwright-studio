Character animation: put TRANSPARENT PNG cut-outs of your characters here.

  kratos.png                     one picture  -> character "kratos"
  kratos/default.png             or a folder of poses named after the character:
  kratos/talk.png                  "talk" = mouth open: it follows the voice-over automatically
  kratos/angry.png                 any other pose: use it with "char_pose": "angry"
  kratos__angry.png              same thing as a single file (name__pose.png)
  kratos/character.json          optional: {"facing": "left"}  (which way the art looks; it is
                                 flipped automatically so it faces the middle of the screen)
  backgrounds/                   optional: images / videos for solo commentary scenes

In segments.json:   "character": "kratos"                       corner over the clip (bottom-right)
                    "character": "kratos", "char_pos": "bottom-left"
                    "commentary": true, "character": "kratos"   no footage -> solo scene, centered, on a background
                    "char_layout": "solo", "char_bg": "#222244"  or an image / video file name
Run "Check characters" (2d_characters.bat) to see them in characters\_preview.png.
